Product Design Case Studies

Retention strategy and engagement design — from research to metrics-driven prototypes.

Duolingo — Retention & Engagement Strategy

Research & Strategy

  • Analyzed core user drop-off patterns via cohort analysis
  • Synthesized insights to identify 3 key churn drivers
  • Ideated 3 competing feature concepts targeting each driver

Design & Validation

  • Designed interactive onboarding flow in Figma
  • Validated interaction patterns through prototyping
  • Delivered annotated design spec + product brief for eng handoff

Key Outcomes

  • Metrics-driven: Measured against 7-day retention, adoption, churn impact
  • Systems thinking: Designed engagement loops with feedback cycles
  • Startup skill: Spec ready for rapid eng iteration

Skills Demonstrated

Retention Thinking Cohort Analysis Feature Strategy Prototyping (Figma) Systems Design Metrics-Driven Design

Key Insight & Next Steps

What I learned: Cohort analysis revealed retention risk clusters at specific milestones, but user interviews would add behavioral context — understanding why users drop off, not just when.

Room for improvement: Would A/B test competing onboarding flows with real users to validate which churn driver has highest impact. Establish baseline metrics to measure actual retention lift vs. prototype assumptions.

JPMorgan — Financial UX & Decision Design

Research & Strategy

  • Led market research + competitive analysis for financial workflows
  • Conducted real-time user interviews with target personas
  • Identified behavioral friction: decision paralysis in high-stakes scenarios

Design & Validation

  • Designed engagement system reducing cognitive load through progressive disclosure
  • Built confidence-driving interactions at decision points
  • Iterated based on user feedback; translated research → design system

Key Outcomes

  • User research focus: Real interviews → behavioral insights
  • Systems thinking: Balanced competing user + business needs
  • Startup skill: Fast research → design → validation cycle

Skills Demonstrated

User Research Competitive Analysis Behavioral Design Engagement Loops Complex Domain UX Real-time Iteration

Key Insight & Next Steps

What I learned: Decision paralysis wasn't caused by lack of information — it was information overload + low confidence. Users needed confidence signals (like progressive commitment + trust anchors) more than additional data.

Room for improvement: Would validate with eye-tracking studies to identify exact decision friction points. Then A/B test engagement patterns (micro-interactions, progressive disclosure) to build confidence at key decision moments.

Approach

Both projects center on understanding user behavior and designing engagement systems: research → identify friction → design engagement loops → validate with users → iterate. For a role like Candle focused on daily engagement and retention, this means understanding behavioral psychology, designing for emotional impact, and measuring what actually moves the needle.
